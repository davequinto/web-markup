Tips

I am a self-taught guitarist that has been playing for around 8 years and the lead guitarist of a band called ScrewDrayber. I might not be the most knowledgeable on guitar, I still think that the tips I could share would still be very valuable, especially for people who are still learning the basics of guitar. The tips that are in this page are the tips that I wish I knew when I started learning guitar.

Tip 1: Always Make Sure Your Guitar Is In Tune

Make sure that your guitar is always in tune when practicing. Getting used to the sound of an out of tune guitar would only hinder your progress. I also recommend to learn how to tune your guitar, a budget friendly and easy way is downloading an app on your phone, but a better and more precise way of tuning would be buying a tuner. (An app that I usually use is called GuitarTuna)

Tip 2: Practice With A Metronome

Practicing with a metronome is one of the best tools to use to improve your overall guitar skills. It improves your timing and you could easily hear or feel when you are wrong. Everyone uses this, especially the professionals. (You don't need an actual metronome, you could just search on YouTube your desired tempo / BPM)

Tip 3: Know How To Hold The Pick The Right Way

Knowing how to hold the pick the right way is very important when starting on learning the guitar, because it is very easy to get used to holding it in a wrong way. And changing into another way of holding the pick is hard. There is no one right way in holding the pick, but picking one that is normally used is what I would recommend.

Tip 4: Practice Daily

Practicing daily for a short amount of time is more efficient than practicing once a week for a long period of time. Playing guitar is all about muscle memory, and practicing daily is one of the easiest way to train it. A short 15 minute practice every day would go a very long way.

Tip 5: Be Kind To Yourself

Know that all great guitarist started the way you are starting right now. Take it easy, enjoy the process of learning the guitar. Being kind to yourself is one of the best ways to stay motivated and keep learning the guitar
