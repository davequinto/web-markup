Acoustic or Electric?

If you are entirely new to guitar, the first problem everyone had is choosing between an acoustic or electric guitar. This is an important decision because, although the two instruments are quite similar, you will learn different skills and techniques depending on which one you choose.

Both guitars have its pros and cons, but the important factor in choosing is knowing which genre of music you personally would like to learn.

If your music genre is more rock, funk and metal then I really recommend an electric guitar. And if your music genre is on the side of country, calm, or pop then I would recommend an acoustic guitar.

If you're on a budget you would need to know that buying an electric guitar also means that you are going to buy an amp and a cable.
